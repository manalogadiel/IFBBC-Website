Our approach

Biblical Preaching

At IFBBC, we believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God and the sole authority for our faith and practice. Our preaching is expositional — meaning we walk through books of the Bible, passage by passage, to understand what God has said and how it applies to our lives today.
