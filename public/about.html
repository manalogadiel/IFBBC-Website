Doctrine

Statement of Faith

As a Fundamental Baptist church, we hold to these essential doctrines of the Christian faith.

We believe the Bible is the verbally inspired, inerrant Word of God — the sole authority for faith and practice in the life of every believer and in the church.
We believe in one God, eternally existent in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — equal in nature, attributes, and perfection.
We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, ascension to the Father's right hand, and His personal, visible, pre-millennial return.
We believe salvation is by grace through faith alone in Jesus Christ alone — not by works. All who repent and trust in Him receive forgiveness of sins and eternal life.
We believe the local church is a congregation of baptized believers, voluntarily associated under the Lordship of Jesus Christ, observing the two ordinances of Baptism and the Lord's Supper, and committed to biblical preaching, fellowship, and missions.
We believe in believer's baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith, and in the Lord's Supper as a memorial of Christ's sacrifice — both are ordinances of the church, not means of salvation.
