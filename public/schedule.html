First time?

What to Expect

Here's a simple guide for first-time visitors to our Sunday Worship Service.

1

A warm welcome

From the moment you arrive, our greeters are there to welcome you and help you find your way. You don't need to bring anything — just come as you are.

2

Worship through music

We open each service with praise and worship songs. You're welcome to sing along or simply take it in. Our worship is heartfelt and focused on God.

3

Preaching from the Bible

Our pastors teach expositionally from Scripture — unpacking the text and applying it to everyday life. Bring a Bible if you have one; there are copies available as well.

4

Community time

After service, we encourage everyone to stay, connect, and get to know one another. This is a great time to meet church members and ask any questions you might have.

5

Children are welcome

We love families! Our Kiddos ministry runs during Worship Service so parents can worship while their children receive age-appropriate teaching and activities.

Getting Here

Barangay Inicbulan is located in Bauan, Batangas. From the Bauan town center, head towards Inicbulan barangay and look for Purok Munlawin. You may ask locals for directions to the IFBBC church building.
