Community

Core Groups

A place for everyone at IFBBC — from the youngest to the most experienced. Each group is a community within our church family.

🧒 Kiddos 🎓 Adelphoi 💼 CAYA 🙏 A-Men 🌸 Womisso
🧒

Kiddos

For children

Kiddos is our children's ministry — a safe, fun, and faith-filled space where kids can learn about Jesus, build friendships, and grow in God's Word. We believe every child matters to God and to this church.

Events & Posters

🎓

Adelphoi

For young people

Adelphoi (from the Greek for "brothers") is our youth ministry — for teenagers and young people navigating life and faith. We gather to worship, dig into the Word, and encourage one another to stand firm in Christ.

Events & Posters

💼

CAYA

For young professionals

CAYA (College Age & Young Adults) is for those stepping into adulthood — navigating careers, relationships, and calling. We wrestle with real questions and seek to honor God in every area of our professional and personal lives.

Events & Posters

🙏

A-Men

For men (after young professional stage)

A-Men is a brotherhood for the men of IFBBC — husbands, fathers, workers, and leaders. We gather to sharpen one another in faith, character, and godly manhood, supporting each other through every season of life.

Events & Posters

🌸

Womisso

For women (non-young-professional stage)

Womisso is a sisterhood for the women of IFBBC — celebrating and supporting one another through every stage of womanhood. We study Scripture together, pray, and walk alongside each other in faith, family, and service.
